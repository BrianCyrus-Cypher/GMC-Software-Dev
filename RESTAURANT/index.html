Our Story

Founded in 2019 by Chef and renowned Artist Michael Cyrus, The Luxe Canvas bagan as a small family bistro in the heart of the city. With nothing more than a passion for art and the art in authentic French couisine , Chef Michael transformd it from a modest storefront into culinary destination. Today, The Luxe Canvas stands as a testament to enduring power of quality, inspiration and the boldness of an artist as the brush dances on the canvas with glee. Every dish that leaves our kitchen carries with it the defintion of true art in culinary excellence.
