Our story

A table for
the curious.

The Luxe Canvas began as a small family bistro in the heart of Nairobi. Today, we are still guided by the same belief: a meal should make you feel something.

Our kitchen brings together local farms, classic French technique, and the bold colour of East Africa. We source with intention, cook with patience, and welcome every guest like an old friend.
