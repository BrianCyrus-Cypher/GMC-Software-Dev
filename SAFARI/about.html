About Safari Horizon Tours

From a single Land Rover and a passion for wildlife to one of Kenya's most trusted safari operators - this is our story.

Our Story

Safari Horizon Tours was founded in 2010 by Daniel Kipchoge, a former Kenya Wildlife Service ranger who spent 15 years protecting elephants in Tsavo National Park. After witnessing thousands of tourists leave Kenya with incomplete or disappointing safari experiences, Daniel decided to create a company that offered something different: authentic, expert-led safaris that puts wildlife and guest experience above everything else.

Starting with just one vehicle and a tented camp in the Maasai Mara, Daniel and his wife Grace grew the company through word of mouth and repeat visitors. Today, Safari Horizon Tours operates across seven national parks and reserves, employs over 60 staff members, and has guided more than 12,000 guests from 45 countries through the Kenyan wilderness.

Despite our growth, we remain a family-owned business committed to the principles Daniel started with: expert guiding, small groups, luxury comfort, and genuine conservation impact.

Our Mission

To provide transformative safari experiences that connect travelers with Kenya's extraordinary wildlife and landscapes while actively contributing to conservation and supporting local communities.

Our Core Values

Wildlife First

Every decision we make prioritizes the well-being of animals and their habitats. We maintain strict ethical viewing distances, never crowd predators, and follow Kenya Wildlife Service guidelines at all times.

Community Empowerment

We employ local Maasai, Samburu, and Kikuyu communities as guides, camp staff, and drivers. We source food from local farms and invest in schools and healthcare facilities in the areas where we operate.

Authentic Experiences

No staged interactions or tourist traps. We take you to the real Kenya - genuine cultural encounters, untouched wilderness, and wildlife moments that no scripted tour can replicate.

Safety Excellence

All our vehicles are equipped with first aid kits, satellite phones, and communication radios. Every guide holds a valid first aid certification and our safety record is 100% since inception.

Meet Our Team

Former Kenya Wildlife Service ranger with 15 years of field experience. Daniel has personally led over 3,000 safaris and is recognized as a KPSGA Silver Level Guide. His encyclopedic knowledge of Kenyan wildlife is unmatched.

Born into a Maasai family in the Mara, Joseph holds the prestigious KPSGA Gold Level certification. With 12 years of guiding experience and an incredible eye for wildlife, Joseph can spot a leopard from half a kilometer away.

A wildlife biologist from the University of Nairobi, Amina manages our conservation programs and community partnerships. She coordinates our anti-poaching support, reforestation projects, and school outreach programs.
