Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a visa to visit Kenya?

Most nationalities require a visa. You can apply for an e-Visa online at evisa.go.ke before your trip. We provide visa assistance information in your pre-departure pack.

Is Kenya safe for tourists?

Yes. Kenya is one of Africa's most popular tourist destinations with a well-established tourism infrastructure. Our guides are trained in safety protocols and we maintain constant communication with park authorities.

What vaccinations do I need?

Yellow fever vaccination is required if arriving from an endemic country. We recommend hepatitis A, typhoid, and ensuring your routine vaccinations are up to date. Consult your doctor 6-8 weeks before travel.

What should I pack for a safari?

Neutral-colored clothing (khaki, olive, brown), sturdy walking shoes, sun hat, sunscreen, binoculars, camera with zoom lens, and a light jacket for early morning drives. We send a detailed packing list upon booking.

Can children go on safari?

Absolutely. We offer family-friendly safaris with shorter game drives, kid-oriented guides, and educational activities. Children under 3 travel free. Ages 3-11 receive a 50% discount.

What is the best time to visit Kenya?

Year-round is great, but the dry seasons (June-October and January-February) offer the best wildlife viewing. The Great Migration in the Mara peaks from July to October.
