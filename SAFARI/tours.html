Mara Classic Safari - 3 Days

Price: $1,200 per person | Duration: 3 Days / 2 Nights | Destination: Maasai Mara

Perfect for first-time safari-goers or those with limited time. The Maasai Mara delivers incredible wildlife density year-round.

Itinerary

  1. Day 1 - Nairobi to Maasai Mara: Depart Nairobi at 7:00 AM by private 4x4 Land Cruiser. The scenic 5-hour drive passes through the Great Rift Valley with a stop at the viewpoint. Arrive at Mara Explorer Camp for lunch. Afternoon game drive (3:00 PM - 6:30 PM) searching for lions, elephants, and giraffes. Sundowner drinks at camp followed by dinner under the stars.
  2. Day 2 - Full Day in the Mara: Early morning game drive (6:00 AM - 9:30 AM) during peak predator activity. Return to camp for breakfast and leisure time. Optional Maasai village visit (cultural experience). Afternoon game drive (3:30 PM - 6:30 PM) focusing on the Mara River hippos and crocodiles. Bush dinner in the wilderness.
  3. Day 3 - Final Game Drive & Return: Dawn game drive (5:45 AM - 8:30 AM) - the best time for photography. Return to camp for full breakfast. Depart for Nairobi arriving by 3:00 PM. Drop-off at your hotel or airport.

What's Included

  • 2 nights luxury tented camp accommodation
  • All meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner)
  • 4 game drives in private 4x4 Land Cruiser
  • Maasai Mara Conservancy entry fees
  • Professional KPSGA-certified guide
  • Round-trip transport from Nairobi
  • Bottled water during game drives

What's Not Included

  • International flights
  • Travel insurance
  • Personal expenses and tips
  • Hot air balloon ride (available as add-on for $450)
  • Visa fees

Big Five Expedition - 5 Days

Price: $2,450 per person | Duration: 5 Days / 4 Nights | Destinations: Maasai Mara & Lake Nakuru

Our most popular tour. Designed to maximize your chances of spotting all Big Five animals: lion, leopard, elephant, rhino, and buffalo.

Itinerary

  1. Day 1 - Nairobi to Lake Nakuru: Depart Nairobi at 8:00 AM. Drive through the Rift Valley to Lake Nakuru National Park (3 hours). Afternoon game drive focusing on the famous flamingo flocks and both black and white rhinos. Overnight at Lake Nakuru Sopa Lodge.
  2. Day 2 - Lake Nakuru Full Day: Full day of game drives around the lake. Visit Makalia Falls. Look for endangered Rothschild giraffes, leopards in the acacia woodland, and buffalo herds. Sundowner at Lion Hill viewpoint. Overnight at lodge.
  3. Day 3 - Nakuru to Maasai Mara: Morning game drive in Nakuru, then scenic drive to the Maasai Mara (5 hours). Afternoon game drive upon arrival. Check in to Mara Big Five Camp. Evening game drive looking for leopard in the riverine forest.
  4. Day 4 - Full Day in the Mara: Early morning and afternoon game drives. Focus on finding the remaining Big Five members. Visit the Mara River crossing points. Optional walking safari with armed ranger (1 session). Bush dinner experience.
  5. Day 5 - Final Safari & Return to Nairobi: Dawn game drive. Full breakfast at camp. Depart for Nairobi arriving by 3:00 PM. Airport or hotel drop-off.

What's Included

  • 4 nights mid-range tented lodge accommodation
  • All meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner)
  • 8 game drives in private 4x4 Land Cruiser
  • All park and conservancy entry fees
  • Professional KPSGA-certified guide
  • 1 walking safari session with armed ranger
  • Maasai village cultural visit
  • Round-trip transport from Nairobi

Amboseli Kilimanjaro Safari - 4 Days

Price: $1,950 per person | Duration: 4 Days / 3 Nights | Destination: Amboseli National Park

Wake up to the sight of Mount Kilimanjaro towering over herds of elephants. Amboseli offers some of the most iconic photo opportunities in Africa.

Itinerary

  1. Day 1 - Nairobi to Amboseli: Depart at 8:00 AM. Scenic drive through Maasai land (4 hours). Arrive for lunch at Kibo Safari Camp with Kilimanjaro views. Afternoon game drive among elephant herds with the mountain as backdrop.
  2. Day 2 - Full Day Amboseli: Early morning game drive (6:00 AM) for Kilimanjaro sunrise photos. Mid-morning visit to Observation Tower for panoramic park views. Afternoon game drive through the dried lake bed looking for cheetahs. Cultural visit to a Maasai boma (homestead).
  3. Day 3 - Amboseli Deep Dive: Full day of game drives covering all sectors of the park. Visit Enkongo Narok Swamp for hippos and waterbirds. Sundowner with Kilimanjaro views. Night game drive (optional, subject to park rules).
  4. Day 4 - Final Morning & Return: Dawn game drive. Breakfast at camp. Depart for Nairobi arriving by 2:00 PM. Hotel or airport drop-off.

What's Included

  • 3 nights luxury tented camp with Kilimanjaro views
  • All meals
  • 6 game drives in private 4x4 Land Cruiser
  • Amboseli National Park entry fees
  • Maasai boma cultural visit
  • Professional KPSGA-certified guide
  • Round-trip transport from Nairobi

Grand Kenya Safari - 7 Days

Price: $3,800 per person | Duration: 7 Days / 6 Nights | Destinations: Amboseli, Lake Nakuru, Maasai Mara, Lake Naivasha

Our flagship tour. Experience Kenya's four most iconic destinations with a hot air balloon ride over the Mara. The ultimate African safari.

Itinerary

  1. Day 1 - Nairobi to Amboseli: Morning drive to Amboseli. Afternoon game drive with Kilimanjaro backdrop. Overnight at luxury camp.
  2. Day 2 - Amboseli Full Day: Two game drives. Elephant encounters and Maasai cultural visit.
  3. Day 3 - Amboseli to Lake Nakuru: Drive to Nakuru via Rift Valley viewpoint. Afternoon game drive. Flamingos and rhinos.
  4. Day 4 - Nakuru to Lake Naivasha: Morning game drive in Nakuru. Transfer to Lake Naivasha. Afternoon boat ride to Crescent Island for a walking safari among giraffes and zebras.
  5. Day 5 - Naivasha to Maasai Mara: Morning at Hell's Gate National Park (cycling and walking safari). Afternoon transfer to the Maasai Mara. Evening game drive.
  6. Day 6 - Maasai Mara Full Day: Hot air balloon ride at dawn (included). Full breakfast in the bush. Afternoon game drive. Farewell bush dinner under the stars.
  7. Day 7 - Final Game Drive & Return: Dawn game drive. Full breakfast. Scenic drive back to Nairobi arriving by 3:00 PM.

What's Included

  • 6 nights luxury tented camp and lodge accommodation
  • All meals including special bush dinners
  • 12 game drives across 4 destinations
  • All park and conservancy entry fees
  • Hot air balloon safari over the Maasai Mara
  • Lake Naivasha boat ride and Crescent Island walking safari
  • Hell's Gate cycling experience
  • 2 cultural village visits (Maasai & Samburu)
  • Professional KPSGA Gold Level guide
  • Round-trip transport from Nairobi
