Safari Photo Gallery

Explore the stunning wildlife, landscapes, and moments captured during our safaris. All photographs were taken by our guides and guests during real tours.

Wildlife

Mara Lion Pride - A dominant male lion resting in the golden grass of the Maasai Mara during the dry season.

Elephant Family - A matriarch leading her herd across the Amboseli plains with Mount Kilimanjaro in the background.

Cheetah on the Hunt - A female cheetah scanning the plains for prey in the early morning light of the Mara.

Giraffe at Sunset - A Rothschild giraffe drinking from a waterhole at Lake Nakuru during golden hour.

Tree Leopard - A leopard resting on a branch in the Mara's riverine forest. One of the rarest Big Five sightings.

Cape Buffalo Herd - A large herd of Cape buffalo cooling off at a watering hole in Amboseli National Park.

Zebra Migration - Thousands of plains zebras grazing during the Great Migration in the northern Mara.

Hippo Pod - A pod of hippos surfacing in the Mara River near the famous crossing points.

Landscapes

Mara Sunrise - The first light breaking over the Maasai Mara grasslands, painting the sky in orange and pink.

Great Rift Valley - Aerial view of the Great Rift Valley escarpment, the geological wonder that created Kenya's lakes.

Kilimanjaro from Amboseli - Africa's tallest peak towering over the Amboseli plains. Best viewed at dawn.

Flamingo Lake - Millions of lesser flamingos turning the shores of Lake Nakuru into a pink ribbon visible from space.

Savanna Sunset - Iconic flat-topped acacia trees silhouetted against a dramatic Kenyan sunset.

Lake Naivasha - A serene view of Lake Naivasha with hippos and waterbirds at dawn.

Camps & Lodges

Mara Explorer Camp - Our flagship luxury tented camp in the heart of the Maasai Mara.

Tent Interior - En-suite luxury tent with king-size bed, mosquito netting, and private veranda overlooking the bush.

Bush Dining - Our outdoor dining area where guests enjoy gourmet meals under the African stars.

Lodge Pool - Cool off at our Amboseli lodge pool with views of Mount Kilimanjaro.

Safari Experiences

Hot Air Balloon Safari - Floating silently over the Mara at dawn during the Great Migration.

Walking Safari - Exploring the bush on foot with our Maasai guides. Tracks, plants, and animals up close.

Maasai Cultural Visit - Visiting a traditional Maasai boma to learn about their customs, dances, and way of life.

Sundowner Game Drive - Ending the day with drinks and snacks at a scenic viewpoint as the sun sets over the savanna.
