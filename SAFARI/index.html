Why Choose Safari Horizon Tours?

Expert Guides

Our guides are born and raised in the Kenyan wilderness. They hold certified Kenya Professional Safari Guides Association (KPSGA) licenses and know every trail, watering hole, and wildlife migration route.

Small Group Sizes

We limit our safari groups to a maximum of 8 guests per vehicle. This ensures intimate wildlife encounters, better photo opportunities, and a personalized experience you won't get with large tour operators.

Luxury Tented Camps

Sleep under the stars in our handpicked luxury tented camps. Each camp features en-suite bathrooms, hot showers, gourmet dining, and front-row seats to the African bush.

Conservation Commitment

10% of every booking goes directly to Kenyan wildlife conservation programs. When you travel with us, you help protect elephants, lions, rhinos, and the communities that live alongside them.

Popular Destinations

Maasai Mara National Reserve

Home to the Great Wildebeest Migration and the Big Five. The Mara offers year-round spectacular wildlife viewing across vast open grasslands.

View Mara Tours

Lake Nakuru National Park

Famous for its massive flocks of pink flamingos, Lake Nakuru is also a sanctuary for endangered black and white rhinos and Rothschild giraffes.

View Nakuru Tours

Amboseli National Park

Set against the stunning backdrop of Mount Kilimanjaro, Amboseli is renowned for its large elephant herds and panoramic views.

View Amboseli Tours
